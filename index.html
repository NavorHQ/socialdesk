SocialDesk

A private publishing tool operated by Navor.

SocialDesk is a small application that runs on Navor's own computer. It schedules and publishes Navor's own videos to Navor's own YouTube channel and TikTok account, using the official YouTube Data API and TikTok Content Posting API.

What it does

  • Uploads videos chosen by the account owner, with the title, description, tags and privacy settings the owner picks.
  • Publishes them at the time the owner schedules.
  • Reports whether each upload succeeded.

Who can use it

SocialDesk isn't a public service and isn't offered to other users. It only connects to accounts owned or managed by Navor.

Your data

Everything SocialDesk stores stays on Navor's computer. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
