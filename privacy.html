Privacy Policy

SocialDesk, operated by Navor. Effective October 13, 2026.

SocialDesk is a private tool that runs on Navor's own computer to schedule and publish Navor's own videos to its YouTube channel and TikTok account. This policy explains what data it accesses and what it does with it.

Data SocialDesk accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the account owner's permission, SocialDesk can upload videos to the connected channel and update their details (title, description, tags, privacy, publish time and thumbnail). It reads the channel's ID and name to confirm which channel is connected.
  • TikTok: with the account owner's permission, SocialDesk reads basic account information (username and available privacy options), uploads videos, and checks whether each upload finished.
  • Sign-in tokens: the access and refresh tokens Google and TikTok issue, so SocialDesk can publish at scheduled times.
  • Content to publish: the videos, captions and settings the account owner provides.

SocialDesk doesn't access viewers' data, comments, messages, analytics, contacts or any account other than the ones its owner connects.

How the data is used

Only to upload and publish the owner's videos as the owner instructs, and to report whether publishing succeeded. SocialDesk doesn't use the data for advertising, profiling or analytics. It doesn't sell it, and it doesn't share it with anyone except Google and TikTok, as needed to publish.

Where it's stored

All data stays on Navor's computer. SocialDesk has no servers of its own. Data travels only between that computer and Google's and TikTok's official APIs, over encrypted connections. Sign-in tokens are kept in a file that only the computer's Windows user account can read.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept until the owner deletes it. The owner can delete SocialDesk's local data at any time and revoke its access:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions

Google and YouTube

SocialDesk uses YouTube API Services. By using SocialDesk you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

SocialDesk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok

Posting to TikTok is subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Children

SocialDesk isn't directed to children and doesn't knowingly collect children's data.
